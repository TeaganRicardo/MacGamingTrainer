# Hades I module

The module targets the exact Intel Steam image declared in `Backend/games/hades/module.json.supportedBuild`; `symbols.json` contains only the image-specific symbol RVAs and signatures. It owns its LLDB sidecar, resident Lua, desired settings, command validation, catalog and UI. Hades II runtime and preference data are independent.

## Workbench presentation

Hades I follows Hades II's ordered workbench navigation: compact and expandable Run Data, Common toggles, unified Boons & Effects (selected current-group editing and exact acquisition), Resources & Gathering, Rooms & Rewards, and Management. The seven permanent resources remain visible as direct rows, with a separate native gain action and fishing-point generation. Rooms & Rewards contains native reward generation, ready-exit reward and route selection, a scheduled eligible Thanatos encounter, Chaos Gate and eligible chamber-object generation. [The room/resource capability map](rooms_resources.md) owns native entrypoints, lifecycle and acceptance boundaries. Whole-workbench bilingual search resolves actions, native resource/facility identities and destinations. The visible-order registry drives shortcuts and their settings.

Hades-native owners determine each capability. [Combat and boon effects](combat_effects.md), [rooms/resources](rooms_resources.md), [management](management-parity.md) and the [Save Editor](save_editor.md) have separate source-backed maps.

## Supported actions

| Domain | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Combat | Invincibility, full life, unlimited Cast/gun ammunition, native reroll permissions, non-consuming Obols/rerolls, full God Gauge while an Aid is available, outgoing/incoming damage and movement/attack-speed multipliers, additional dashes |
| Game speed | Game-native simulation-time multiplier 0–10× through `AdjustSimulationSpeed`, separate from resident feature preferences and composed with native `SimSpeedChanges` |
| Boon rarity | On/off selectable Common/Rare/Epic/Heroic minimum for future normal Olympian/Hermes offers and rerolls; preserves higher native/Legendary odds and does not alter eligibility, fixed rarity, existing offers, Chaos, Hammer or Pom selection |
| Hero values | Set health, maximum health, Obols, rerolls and inactive God Gauge; fixed Health current/max, Obol and reroll locks use the shared Hades II lock affordance |
| Permanent resources | Edit the seven native resource owners declared by `schema.py` and the generated catalog |
| Gain and gathering | Add native resources/Obols with their bookkeeping; generate an eligible native fishing point and retain normal fishing/catch/turn-in behavior |
| Rooms and rewards | Generate native loot/pickups, select eligible ready-exit rewards/routes while preserving potential native forced choices, schedule an eligible Thanatos encounter, generate Chaos Gate/Trove/Well/Purge objects using available native points, restore an owned unchanged exit before entry |
| Acquisition | Search the official bilingual reward catalog; acquire Olympian/Hermes boons, weapon-compatible Hammers, Chaos blessings, Well/NPC effects including Patroclus rewards, Keepsakes and Companions through their native owners |
| Current boons | Observe current native effects; selected group level/rarity/removal, Chaos pair activation/cancellation, physical effect uses/expiry/cooldown and native equipment rank/lifecycle actions |
| Management | Game-scoped customizable shortcuts, feature profiles, disable all, current/history logs and diagnostics/export; manual backups, stopped-game restore and staged restore through Core Save; independent active/verified-backup [Save Editor](save_editor.md) with direct domains and typed Advanced edits |

Multipliers accept 0.1–100 and additional dashes accept 0–20. Additional dashes preserve the native weapon's existing charges. Non-consuming operations preserve actual gains and native purchase/reroll bookkeeping. Direct numeric edits remain intentional absolute edits. Fixed Health locks capture the current/max pair; native damage, healing and max-health recalculation still run, after which the pair is reasserted. Hearts, Mirror health, curses and trait multipliers therefore keep changing the native max-health owner underneath the lock, and unlock/Profile clear/Disable All return authority to that native result without retroactive healing. Exact health/max edits while locked advance the durable pair. Maximum-health edits commit their adjustment through the native `RoomRewardEmptyMaxHealthTrait` contribution, preserving it when later native rewards or traits recalculate maximum health. Fixed Obol/reroll locks remain distinct from Infinite Gold/Infinite Rerolls and preserve native spend/gain accounting. The God Gauge control preserves the Aid's native active duration. The rarity toggle is off by default and its minimum target defaults to Heroic. Common preserves natural rarity; Rare/Epic/Heroic raise only the tiers needed to establish that minimum while the native synchronous SetTraitsOnLoot selection is running. The original odds are restored even on failure, higher native and Legendary probability stays intact, and original offer gating is preserved. It does not edit game files or save a forced rarity into existing loot objects. Its behavior is grounded in the supported-build Content/Scripts/TraitScripts.lua (SHA256 473b3a2df05923bdbfe123e35047ff86ee44db4c7f4e3881e1812461d43ee3a6) and RoomManager.lua (SHA256 ca379fe0708af778007aef929822e4cfe8cf3a47dd2a3d37bf57e582ddb1b924).

Hades I stores an upgraded boon as several same-name trait records while presenting one boon in its HUD. A current-boon row therefore represents that native group. Its identity includes the actual current group members; replacing the group invalidates the old token. Mixed-rarity and special-owner effects report a concrete lifecycle limitation. They do not authorize deleting unrelated effects by a stale name.

Acquiring a Keepsake or Companion uses its native equipment owner, replaces the previous item and records the selected rarity's progression. Lucky Tooth receives its native extra life; Companions receive their native assist weapon. Reacquiring the same equipped item and rarity preserves consumed uses. Changing the same Keepsake's rank preserves earned dodge/damage bonuses, consumed uses and current-room state; changing the same Companion's rank preserves consumed charges and applies the native charge difference for the rank change. Ordinary Keepsake rank maps to native chamber thresholds; Companion rank maps to the selected assist's upgrade record. Narrative requirements are not fabricated.

## Ownership and recovery

The game-speed control runs on the existing exact-build Hades Lua bridge at the main-thread `App::Update` boundary. It wraps the game's `AdjustSimulationSpeed` function and multiplies each native fraction by the Trainer's durable 0–10× preference. The native fraction and its arguments are retained separately, so native slow/fast effects continue to compose while the Trainer multiplier is active. A manual disconnect restores the Trainer multiplier to 1× before detaching while preserving any active native effect. An unknown mutation outcome retains the same-process recovery interlock. Lua fixtures verify composition and recovery; actual gameplay-wide timing, 0× freeze and frame pacing remain separate native acceptance evidence.

Durable desired settings live under the module's own `MacGamingTrainer/hades` directory. Reconnecting applies them to a compatible runtime; hero setup remounts them after native per-room state resets. Manual detach preserves desired intent. Disable All and application termination reset desired settings and attempt resident cleanup. Offline Disable All reports that in-game cleanup needs reconnection or a game restart. A failed preference write is reported while resident cleanup still runs. Native scripted movement temporarily owns speed; its completion or native reset remounts the current movement multiplier. Committed numeric edits remain after Disable All.

Reconnect compares resident fixed-value locks with durable intent, including an explicit unlocked document, and releases stale resident locks. A same-process Lua reset restores acknowledged locks and the rarity target with the continuous feature settings. A live reply reports resident lock state; a detached reply presents durable intent. Captured Health current/max pairs retain finite native fractional values and their ordering through preferences and Profiles. Explicit numeric edits retain their integer input contract. Damage-multiplier activity requires the current Hero/object identity and the actual mounted Trainer modifier, value and multiplicative mode; a status read does not remount a missing or replaced effect.

One-shot requests carry the observed hero identity and, for existing boons, the selected group token. Room/door actions carry the observed native room and selected door/reward tokens. The worker verifies these owners inside the same Lua execution before mutation. A draft captured for an earlier hero cannot be applied to its replacement, including a fresh Lua runtime in the same game process. Focused number drafts survive observations; unfocused fields follow current values. Pending encounter intents remain resident-only and cannot be replayed from profiles or saves.

Trait edits are submitted once and first resumed by the game's native coroutine scheduler. Acquisition/replacement and expiry transformations close an open native trait-detail menu before changing records or modifiers. Existing group tokens are checked again after that yielding teardown. Continuations retain their original run, hero, room and menu owner. Pending work may be cancelled only with a confirmed not-started receipt; started or unconfirmed actions require restart recovery and are never replayed. The [save-corruption investigation](../../development/hades1-boon-save-corruption.md) records the execution defect, native missing-body save path and pending isolated save/load acceptance.

The backend records an in-flight native request before sending it. A lost acknowledgement or backend crash retains that record. Reconnecting to the same process lifetime requires restarting the game; the request is never replayed automatically. A new process lifetime clears the obsolete marker. The UI revokes mutation authority when the runtime outcome or protocol is untrustworthy.

One execution cleanup owner observes suspension before deleting the private debugger target's breakpoints, restoring focus and releasing scratch memory. A failed boundary retains its breakpoint in that target until cleanup or detach. Cleanup failure remains an unknown outcome, including when the original native call also failed; it cannot erase the recovery journal or authorize another command.

An LLDB expression-engine interruption can bypass Lua VM cleanup. A scratch-memory execution phase distinguishes failures before Lua entry and after completed cleanup from interruption inside Lua; an unreadable phase is unresolved. For an interrupted or unresolved Lua call, the transport force quits its owned game attachment before other cleanup, requires an observed exit, and reports lost unsaved progress. If termination fails, it retains the debugger, blocks resume/detach and instructs immediate manual Force Quit; close retries termination without another native reset. Ordinary protected Lua errors do not trigger this policy.

The debugger child owns its native shutdown cleanup. Client close or terminal reply loss delivers EOF without killing that worker; its EOF cleanup retains the interrupted attachment and retries bounded termination attempts until the game has exited. Normal disconnect still releases native owners before the child exits. This lifetime policy is specific to the Hades debugger sidecar.

The resident revision and source path are declared in `module.json` and extracted by the existing shared revision/provenance tooling. A different or legacy resident requires a game restart. Runtime-only tables and temporary Trainer projections must be excluded from native saves.

The world-definition compatibility identity is a canonical SHA-256 of the exact definitions supplied by the game-owned catalog adapter. Object-key ordering and presentation-only names do not change that identity. An existing resident with different or unidentified definitions requires restarting the game before another runtime command; definitions and pending world actions are never replaced in place. The current runtime change also requires restarting any game that still contains the preceding resident revision. Product version, module protocol and supported-game identity are independent of this revision.

The Hades UTF-8 request bound is owned by `LUA_SOURCE_LIMIT_BYTES` in `transport.py`. At historical integration source `66b19638bf5401651caa2f7d3773902d74650f54`, static construction measured 65,006 bytes for default connect/configure and 65,724 for a selected level-100 native boon group with instance IDs 1,000,000–1,000,099. The latter exceeded the former 64 KiB bound and motivated its increase; subsequent runtime changes alter those request sizes. The worker builds Lua after the bounded JSONL parameter request; the native transport allocates from the encoded byte length and passes a pointer plus `unsigned long` length to `luaL_loadbufferx`. `tests/test_hades_transport_cleanup.py` covers the current actual builders against the production bound, an exact-limit request reaching the boundary, and limit-plus-one rejection before game access.

## Native evidence and validation

`catalog.json.sourceEvidence` records the exact native data and official bilingual text hashes. `Tools/generate_hades_catalog.py` reproduces the acquisition catalog and current-trait name registry; `Tools/generate_hades_world_catalog.py` reproduces the separate room/reward/facility registry and its native source hashes. Native sources establish `SetupHeroObject` room resets, `SpendMoney`/`AddMoney` bookkeeping, native property application, the three reroll entry points, and trait acquisition/teardown. `Main.lua:Save` serializes non-ignored global tables; the module excludes its resident state and transient projections.

The test sources cover native-shaped Lua calls, yielding reroll lifecycles, remount/reset, gains, hero/group/room ownership and crash recovery. The macOS frontend harness compiles the actual Core + Hades Swift graph and exercises AppKit number fields, focused drafts, target changes, bilingual catalog data, shortcuts and fatal-error recovery. Temporary filesystem cases cover save membership, feature profiles and Core backup/restore integration. Executed automation evidence belongs to each exact-source PR; [PROJECT_STATUS.md](../../../PROJECT_STATUS.md) owns operating limits and manual acceptance.

Development game launches use an isolated `CFFIXED_USER_HOME`, with an OS sandbox denying access to the real Hades save directory. The pre-audit native session on source `22c83b52dda6450de8b7f82a84fe890a49b8f002` preserved all 15 original file hashes and completed 133 requests without errors, including 100 consecutive status requests, hero and seven-resource edits, purchase bookkeeping that preserves gains, boon group acquisition/update/removal, Lucky Tooth's extra life, Companion weapon registration and consumed-use preservation. An already-running native God Gauge drain coroutine remained at the native cap after enabling the control. The native save payload excluded resident tables and temporary Trainer invulnerability/damage projections, while live invulnerability was restored. Room reload retained movement/dash projections; Disable All restored their observed native baselines and removed Trainer invulnerability.

The subsequent whole-module audit found seven defects tracked in [#568](https://github.com/TeaganRicardo/MacGamingTrainer/issues/568), including native maximum-health ownership and completed landing recovery that this earlier pass missed. Corrections were independently reviewed and merged through PR #569. The [resumed native packet](https://github.com/TeaganRicardo/MacGamingTrainer/issues/568#issuecomment-6412086230) on exact source `b35f9e72d9caa66968ec80b8d842272c7166219a` records 35 direct worker/runtime requests: successful scripted arrival, an actual room transition, two menu-driven checkpoint reloads, native health recalculation, later reward-authored persistence, stale-target rejection and cleanup. All 15 real user files remained unchanged. This focused historical packet does not establish the integrated batch's behavior. Full combat/HUD, attack timing, real reroll menus, every acquisition category, Trainer rendering/global shortcuts/accessibility and Host reconnect/quit remain in consolidated acceptance. Earlier development sessions on macOS 27.0.1 with Xcode 27.1 exited with a Rosetta translation-removal assertion; its cause remains unconfirmed in [#565](https://github.com/TeaganRicardo/MacGamingTrainer/issues/565).
