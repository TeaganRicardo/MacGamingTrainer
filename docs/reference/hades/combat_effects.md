# Hades I Common and Boons & Effects capabilities

This is the source-backed coverage map for [#571](https://github.com/TeaganRicardo/MacGamingTrainer/issues/571), under [#124](https://github.com/TeaganRicardo/MacGamingTrainer/issues/124). Compatibility is owned by `Backend/games/hades/module.json`; the extracted native script and official bilingual text hashes are recorded in `catalog.json.sourceEvidence`. The supported native owners below are relative to the game's `Content/Scripts/` directory. They were inspected read-only; extraction loads data chunks rather than the game.

Resources, NPC rewards, exits and rooms belong to [#572](https://github.com/TeaganRicardo/MacGamingTrainer/issues/572). Profiles, diagnostics and Save Editor belong to [#573](https://github.com/TeaganRicardo/MacGamingTrainer/issues/573) and [#584](https://github.com/TeaganRicardo/MacGamingTrainer/issues/584). Their completion is tracked by those owners.

Native limitations remain visible on the current effect row. A mixed-rarity group, or separately owned same-name groups whose damage/slot/weapon overrides are keyed only by name, cannot be rewritten without crossing another owner: `RemoveTraitData` itself removes these modifiers by name. Those specific edit/removal actions stay unavailable. A setup function, weapon enchantment or pending expiry is managed through its supported lifecycle actions; generic level/rarity rewriting is unavailable when it would discard that lifecycle. Unknown native effects can be displayed using the terminology registry but cannot be acquired or rewritten merely because they have a localized name.

Hades II's Selene/Hex, Arcana, Arachne, Echo and Familiar mechanics have no matching Hades I native pools/owners in the inspected `LootData`, `TraitData`, `GiftData` and `StoreData`. They are inapplicable. Their Hades I counterparts are represented by God Gauge/Aid, Mirror-derived game rules, native NPC rewards, Keepsakes and Companions rather than copied labels.

## State, recovery and evidence

Durable desired combat settings live in Hades preferences and are replayable at reconnect/hero setup. `activeFeatures` separately reports a live hero and native applicability; movement and Aid can be temporarily unavailable while desired intent remains enabled. Live life, meter, resources, selected trait tokens, uses and cooldown are observed from the current native owner. Native one-shots use captured hero ownership and the existing unknown-outcome non-replay transport contract. Profiles must replay only durable settings, never acquisitions, expiration, charge changes or rank mutations.

Trainer flags/modifiers and forced door permissions are stripped during native `Save` and restored after its synchronous success or error. Forced exit leases include current run, room and actual target-room identity, so changing a door target cannot restore an obsolete permission. Saved gameplay effects and deliberate progression edits remain native game state. Resident revision 4 requires restarting a game that still hosts an older revision. Persisted schema 1 migrates only the three exact shipped historical preference key sets, adding neutral defaults for new settings.

Owning contracts were authored in `tests/test_hades_combat_parity.py`, `test_hades_runtime.py`, `test_hades_catalog.py`, `test_hades_commands.py`, `test_hades_game_speed.py` and the native-control journey in `test_hades_frontend.py`. They cover new successful paths and selected-owner/disable/error/dormant recovery cases. **They have not been executed under the current local test/build/game pause.** The only executable development action was regenerating the frozen catalog from read-only data chunks. No save or live game mutation was performed.

After independent review of the final integrated SHA, the authorized hosted phase must run Linux contracts and the required macOS/build contexts for that exact head. Native gameplay, rendered UI, timing, save/load and user acceptance remain pending; source inspection and authored fixtures do not establish them. `PROJECT_STATUS.md` owns that operating boundary.
